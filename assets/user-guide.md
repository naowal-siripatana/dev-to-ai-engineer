# PumpGuard user guide

## Overview

PumpGuard is a small monitor for water pumps. It reads the pressure and the temperature of the pump every 10 seconds and keeps the readings of the last 90 days. A green light means that everything is normal, and a red light means that there is a problem that needs your attention.

## Installation

Mount the monitor on a flat wall near the pump, at eye level. Use the four screws from the box and do not over-tighten them. Connect the sensor cable first and the power cable last. When the unit is ready, the status light turns green and the screen shows the home page.

## Calibration

Calibrate the sensor once a month, so that the readings stay accurate. Open the menu, choose Calibrate, and keep the pump running at normal speed for two minutes. The monitor stores the new values and shows the date of the last calibration on the home screen. If the last calibration is older than 30 days, the monitor shows the error E107.

## Network setup

Choose Settings, then Network, and pick the name of your Wi-Fi network. Enter the password and wait until the light turns solid green. The monitor works on 2.4 GHz networks only. If the network is lost, the readings are stored on the unit and sent later, when the connection returns.

## Alarms

When the pressure is above 8 bar or the temperature is above 45 degrees Celsius, the light turns red and the alarm sounds. To turn the sound off, open Settings, choose Sound, and set the alarm to Silent. The light still turns red when there is a problem. We do not recommend silencing the alarm on pumps that run without anyone nearby.

## Maintenance

Wipe the casing with a dry cloth every month. Do not use water or cleaning spray near the sensor port. Check the sensor cable for cracks every three months and replace it if the outer layer is damaged. The battery lasts about three years and must be replaced by trained staff only.

## Troubleshooting

The error E101 means that the sensor did not answer: check the sensor cable and restart the monitor. The error E102 means that the battery is low: connect the power cable and charge the monitor overnight. The error E103 means that the monitor is too hot: move it away from heat and let it cool down. The error E104 means that the storage is above 95 percent: export your readings and delete the old ones.

## Warranty

The monitor has a two year warranty on manufacturing faults. The warranty does not cover water damage or a cracked sensor cable. Keep your receipt and the serial number, which is on the label under the device.
