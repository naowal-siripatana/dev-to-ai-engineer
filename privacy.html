Privacy

This page explains what the "My Learning" area of this site stores and why. It is a draft written in plain language; please read it and ask if anything is unclear.

What we store

  • Your name and email address from your Google account, when you sign in.
  • The date you first signed in (your Day 1), your time zone, and whether you confirmed you subscribed to the channel.
  • Which checklist items you have ticked.
  • Whether you agreed to receive learning news by email, and when.

Why

To show your own plan and checklist when you come back, and, only if you tick the box, to send you learning news by email. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Where it is kept

In a database provided by Supabase. Each member can read and change only their own rows. Sign-in is handled by Google; we never see your Google password.

Your choices

  • You can turn email news off at any time in My Learning, and every email will include an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all your data by emailing naowalzaza@gmail.com.

Cookies and storage

Your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay signed in. The site loads fonts and scripts from Google Fonts and jsDelivr.
